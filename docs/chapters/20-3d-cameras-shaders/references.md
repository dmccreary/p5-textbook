# References: 3D Cameras, Lighting Models, Materials & Shaders

1. [Phong reflection model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phong_reflection_model) - Wikipedia - Classical empirical model for calculating ambient, diffuse, and specular illumination of points on a surface in 3D computer graphics for realistic material rendering.

2. [OpenGL Shading Language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenGL_Shading_Language) - Wikipedia - High-level shading language based on C, executing parallel vertex and fragment shader programs directly on graphics processing units (GPUs) for high-performance visual effects.

3. [Texture mapping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texture_mapping) - Wikipedia - Technique for mapping 2D image bitmaps and UV coordinate wraps onto 3D polygon mesh surfaces for rich visual detail and realistic surface texturing.

4. The Book of Shaders - Patricio Gonzalez Vivo and Jen Lowe - Self-Published / Online - Gonzalez Vivo and Lowe revolutionized GPU shader pedagogy with their interactive visual fragment shader platform, teaching procedural coordinate math to digital artists and creative coders.

5. OpenGL Programming Guide: The Official Guide to Learning OpenGL (Ninth Edition) - John Kessenich, Graham Sellers, and Dave Shreiner - Addison-Wesley - The definitive Red Book established industry standards for lighting models, camera view matrices, specular highlights, and vertex attributes in modern OpenGL and WebGL pipelines.

6. [p5.js 3D Lighting and Material Reference](https://p5js.org/reference/#group-3D) - p5.js Foundation - Documentation for ambientLight(), directionalLight(), pointLight(), ambientMaterial(), specularMaterial(), texture(), and createShader() for advanced 3D surface illumination and custom GPU shading in p5.js.

7. [WebGL Shaders and GLSL Fundamentals](https://webglfundamentals.org/webgl/lessons/webgl-shaders-and-glsl.html) - WebGL Fundamentals - Detailed guide explaining GLSL types (vec2, vec3, vec4, mat4), attributes, uniforms, varyings, and vertex-to-fragment pipeline data flow in programmable GPU shaders.

8. [The Coding Train: Custom WebGL Shaders in p5.js](https://thecodingtrain.com/tracks/code-programming-with-p5-js) - The Coding Train - Video series demonstrating how to write vertex and fragment shader files (.vert, .frag) and pass mouse/time uniforms into p5.js shaders for generative graphics.

9. [Learn OpenGL: Basic Lighting](https://learnopengl.com/Lighting/Basic-Lighting) - LearnOpenGL (Joey de Vries) - Acclaimed tutorial explaining diffuse light vectors, normal vectors, specular shininess exponents, and ambient light equations with clear visual diagrams and mathematical derivations.

10. [Shadertoy Beta and Fragment Shader Playground](https://www.shadertoy.com/) - Shadertoy (Inigo Quilez) - World-renowned online community and live GPU code repository demonstrating procedural raymarching, fractals, and lighting in GLSL fragment shaders for creative graphics developers.
