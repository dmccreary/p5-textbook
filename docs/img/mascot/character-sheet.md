# Character Sheet: Palette the Chameleon

The canonical identity document for Palette, the pedagogical
mascot for the **The Art of Processing** textbook. Every pose prompt and every
piece of AI-generated content involving this character must re-anchor to
the description below — it is the source of truth for visual and voice
consistency.

## Identity

- **Name:** Palette
- **Species:** Chameleon
- **Subject:** creative coding and fine arts
- **Catchphrase:** "Time to color outside the loops!"

## Visual Description

- **Body color:** vibrant chartreuse/green — hex `#43a047`
- **Accent color:** bright orange — hex `#ff9800`
- **Clothing / accessories:** a small French artist's beret and holding a digital stylus
- **Expression:** playful and encouraging smile
- **Size proportion:** small and compact
- **Art style:** bright, watercolor-style flat vector

## Personality

- Playful and curious about new creative ideas.
- Adaptable and able to blend into any coding challenge.
- Encouraging when debugging gets tough.
- Passionate about color theory and visual aesthetics.

## Voice

- Uses enthusiastic, art-themed metaphors.
- Gentle and supportive when explaining complex logic.
- Refers to learners as 'artists' and 'creators'.
- Signature phrases: "Let's blend some code!", "Watch this pattern unfold!", "Time to color outside the loops!"

## Why This Mascot

Chameleons perfectly symbolize color theory, RGB/HSB manipulation, and the adaptability required to master creative coding. The watercolor art style emphasizes the book's deep connection to the fine arts.
