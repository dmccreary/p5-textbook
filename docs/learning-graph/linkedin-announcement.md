Excited to share a new open educational resource: an interactive textbook on creative coding and computational thinking designed for students and educators!

"The Art of Processing" combines AI-assisted content generation with proven educational frameworks. Built using my state-of-the-art intelligent textbooks agent skills, it incorporates learning graphs showing concept dependencies, interactive MicroSims using p5.js, and comprehensive assessment tools to make creative coding and computational thinking accessible and engaging.

By the numbers:

- 25 chapters covering 2D/3D graphics, color theory, physics simulation, and audio synthesis
- 600 concepts organized in a dependency graph
- 73 interactive MicroSims (p5.js simulations)
- 250 quiz questions for self-assessment
- 600 glossary terms with precise definitions
- 89 FAQs addressing common student misconceptions
- 220,000+ words (approx. 930 equivalent printed pages)
- 4 appendices on this history of p5 and guides for integrating the textbook into your LMS

Now for the big surprise.  This entire textbook was generated using the latest version Google Antigravity Desktop tools executing my mostly portable intelligent textbooks skills library!
This was a non-trivial process.  Google's LLMs needed a lot more detailed prompts than other frameworks to generate consistent quality content.  This is an ongoing project, but I wanted to
share my first textbook generated with Google LLMs.
