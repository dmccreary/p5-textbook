    Concept to MicroSim Plan¶

    MicroSim: Canvas Inspector

    A foundational interactive grid where to demonstrate understanding of Coordinate System, the student can drag a point to see its (x, y) coordinates update in real-time, adjust sliders for canvas width/height, and see the setup() and draw() loop execution counts increment.

    Controls: - Coordinate Dragger - Width/Height Sliders - Frame Rate Slider - Background Color Picker
